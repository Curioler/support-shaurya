# The steroid clock

**Updated:** 2026-08-15
**Basis:** [Dr Shiji Chalipat, 25 May 2026](../sources/2026-05-25-dr-shiji-chalipat-prescription.md)

Corticosteroids are standard DMD care, but they are also an **entry requirement** for the
trials that fit Shaurya's genotype. The eligibility clock starts on his first dose, not on
the day anyone applies. This page tracks that clock, because it determines when enquiries
can actually convert.

---

## What was prescribed

**Deflazacort 12 mg, once daily**, to start **6 weeks from 25 May 2026** → around
**6 July 2026**.

At the weight recorded that day, 14.74 kg, that is **≈0.81 mg/kg/day**.

That number matters. Trials specify a minimum steroid intensity, and SGT-003 asks for
**≥0.75 mg/kg/day of deflazacort**. The prescribed dose clears it. Had it been lower, the
prescription itself would have been an eligibility problem.

## Why the six-week wait was correct

Dr Joshi was holding deflazacort pending sight of the vaccination card. Dr Chalipat's note
explains it: Shaurya had **already had flu and varicella vaccines**, and further flu and
pneumococcal vaccines were planned. **Varicella is a live vaccine, and live vaccines must
be given before immunosuppression begins.** The delay was clinical sequencing, not
drift — worth knowing, because from outside it can look like time being lost.
