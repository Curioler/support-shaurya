# Standard patient summary — plain text

**Why this exists.** The email to Prof. Acharya used markdown tables and bullets. They
arrived merged and unreadable, and his first reply was not about Shaurya at all — it was
"columns merged so have trouble understanding".

That is a wasted exchange with someone whose time we were already asking for. The same
mistake with a trial coordinator at Dyne or Precision would be worse: they receive many
enquiries, and an unreadable one gets set aside rather than queried.

**So: paste the block below as plain text.** No tables, no markdown, no bold, no bullet
characters beyond simple hyphens. It renders identically in Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and
on a phone.

## Notes on use

- **For trial coordinators**, lead with the three lines that determine routing: out-of-frame
  exon 50 deletion, ambulant, age 4 years 7 months. Then paste the block below it.
- **For Prof. Acharya**, the block plus the MedGenome PDF is exactly what he asked for.
- **Keep it current.** Weight, age and steroid status change. Update this file, not a copy
  in someone's sent folder.
- **The two "not established" lines are deliberate.** The AAV antibody status and the missing
  functional measures are precisely what a specialist will ask about, and stating them as
  gaps is more credible than omitting them.

## A consent point before the report goes further

Prof. Acharya wrote: *"I shall, in confidence request an ex-student, to explain more."*
