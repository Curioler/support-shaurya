My name is Gaurang K. Nayak, and I am writing this with a father's
heart, filled with fear and hope at the same time.

My son Shaurya is four years old. At an age when children should be
running, laughing and starting school, my little boy is fighting
Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy - a rare genetic disease that slowly takes
away the strength of every muscle in the body, including the heart and
the muscles used to breathe.

On 1st May 2026, our world changed. A blood test showed his CPK level
was 22,051, against a normal range of 75 to 230 for his age. Genetic
testing at MedGenome Labs, Bangalore then confirmed what we were
dreading: Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy, caused by a deletion in the
dystrophin gene.

Today Shaurya still walks. He still climbs stairs, though it is
becoming harder, and he needs to push on his knees to stand up from the
floor. Every day that passes, this disease takes a little more.

Shaurya is under the care of Dr Ann Agnes Mathew, Consultant Paediatric
Neurologist at SPARSH Hospital, Bengaluru, alongside doctors in
Vadodara and Pune. On 9th July 2026, Dr Mathew prescribed ELEVIDYS
(delandistrogene moxeparvovec), a one-time gene therapy that offers a
real chance of slowing this disease.

In her written certificate, she says something that will not leave me:

"I recommend that the above-mentioned medicine be imported for saving
the life of this patient as soon as possible, as the medicine works in
a time limited fashion and works better if given earlier."

That is why we cannot wait. This treatment works best while a child can
still walk. For Shaurya, that window is open now.

ELEVIDYS is not manufactured in India and must be imported. The cost is
approximately US$3.2 million - around Rs. 26 crore, excluding taxes and
import duties.

I work in fire and safety, and I am the only earning member of our
family. We have already spent everything we had on diagnostic tests and
on the physiotherapy Shaurya needs every week. Rs. 26 crore is a number
I cannot even picture. But next to my son's life, it is only a number.

So today I am setting aside my pride and asking for your help.

Every contribution matters, whether it is Rs. 500 or Rs. 5,000. And if
you cannot give, please share our fundraiser with your family, friends
and colleagues. One share can reach the one person who is able to help.

All our medical documents - the genetic report, the doctors'
prescriptions and the treating physician's certificate - have been
submitted and verified on this platform. We have nothing to hide. We
only have a son we are trying to save.

Thank you for reading this far, and for standing with us.

With gratitude,
