# Questions for Gaurang

Everything that only the family can answer, in the order worth asking.

**A note on how to use this.** Gaurang is a father four months into his son's diagnosis,
running two fundraisers and dealing with clinicians in three cities. A numbered list of
fifteen questions will read as an audit, however kindly meant. **Send Round 1 only.** The
rest can follow once he has answered, or come up naturally in conversation.

These four decide whether everything else is worth doing. Ready to paste:

## Round 2 — test results already ordered

Ask once Round 1 is answered.

- The **2D Echo with GLS** Dr Chalipat ordered on 25 May — was it done? What did it show?
- The tests ordered for **Jolly bhabhi** (CPK, DMD-MLPA, 2D Echo) — done? Results?
- Were the **flu and pneumococcal vaccines** given?
- MedGenome recommended **sequencing of exon 50** to confirm the deletion. Has that been
  done? *(Trial sponsors check genotype through their own genetic counsellors, and an
  unconfirmed single-exon result is the sort of thing that stalls an application.)*

## Round 3 — measurements to request at the next clinic visit

Not questions so much as a request. These are ordinary things his clinic already knows how
to measure, and **trial sites ask for them by name**:

- **NSAA** (North Star Ambulatory Assessment) total score — PBGENE-DMD needs 16–29
- **Time to rise from the floor** — FORZETTO needs under 10 seconds
- **10 metre walk/run time**
- Current **weight and height**

Their absence is the difference between an enquiry a coordinator can act on and one they
put aside.

  before any of this goes on a website.

  addresses on the original list were undeliverable, so silence may mean the email never

## Things to tell him, not ask

Worth leading with these — it makes the questions feel like part of a shared effort rather
than a checklist:

- **Shaurya's mutation is one of the most treatable in DMD.** The exon 50 deletion means
  exon 51 skipping applies, which is the most developed route in the whole disease — most
  drugs, most trials, only approvals.
- **A Phase 3 trial matches his exact mutation** (Dyne's FORZETTO), and a gene-editing trial
  takes his exact age and mutation range (Precision's FUNCTION-DMD).
- **Dr Chalipat's own plan says to explore trials worldwide** — so this is following medical
  advice, not going around it.
- **The two campaigns have raised ₹11,51,321 between them.** Neither page shows that, and
  the combined figure is worth announcing.
