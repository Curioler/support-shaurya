## Why exon 50 is a fortunate deletion to have

A deletion of exon 50 alone shifts the reading frame, which is why it produces Duchenne
rather than the milder Becker. Skipping the *neighbouring* exon 51 restores the frame and
lets the body make a shortened but working dystrophin.

**Exon 51 skipping is the single most-developed therapeutic route in all of DMD.** It is
the target the field went after first, so it has the most drugs, the most trials, and the
only approvals. Of all the mutations Shaurya could have had, this is among the best served.

### ELEVIDYS is unaffected

ELEVIDYS works regardless of which exon is deleted, with one exception: its label
contraindicates it for patients with deletions in exon 8 and/or exon 9. Exon 50 is not
affected, so **the ₹26 crore route remains genotype-valid.** ⚠ Confirm against the current
package insert.

## Approved exon-51 drug

**Eteplirsen (EXONDYS 51)** holds FDA accelerated approval specifically for exon 51
skipping. It is a weekly infusion rather than a one-time therapy, its benefit is debated,
and it is not approved in India or the EU. It is not a substitute for the gene therapy
plan — but it exists, it matches his mutation, and it is worth asking Dr Joshi about,
including whether any access or compassionate-use route reaches India.

## The most time-sensitive thing in this document

**As of 21 May 2026, deflazacort had not been started.** Dr Joshi was waiting on the
vaccination card before beginning it.

Corticosteroids are not just standard DMD care — they are an **entry requirement** for the
trials that fit:

- **SGT-003** needs ≥12 weeks of stable steroids before screening
- **FORZETTO** needs ≥24 weeks of stable glucocorticoids before randomisation

The consequence is worth stating plainly: **the eligibility clock does not start when the
family applies to a trial. It starts on the day Shaurya takes his first dose of
deflazacort.** If he began treatment in, say, June 2026, he reaches the 12-week mark
around September 2026 and the 24-week mark around December 2026. If the vaccination card
is still outstanding and steroids have not begun, then every week of delay pushes both
dates back by a week — and one of those trials is a Phase 3 study that will finish
enrolling.

This is the cheapest, most concrete lever in the entire campaign. It costs nothing.
