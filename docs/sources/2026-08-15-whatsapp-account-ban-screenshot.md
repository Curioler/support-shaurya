## What the screen actually tells us

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "This account can't use WhatsApp" | Full account ban, not a temporary restriction |
| "Recent account activity doesn't follow our Terms of Service" | Generic ToS enforcement wording. In practice this phrasing is overwhelmingly used for **bulk / unsolicited messaging** detection |
| **"Requesting a review is not available"** | **The in-app appeal path is closed.** Either a review has already been made and concluded, or the account is in a category that does not offer one. This is the most serious line on the screen |

## The date discrepancy — resolve before writing any appeal

The screen says **8 Aug 2026**; Gaurang understands the account to have been locked on
**15 Aug 2026**. Possible explanations:

1. He submitted a review request on 8 Aug, it was decided, and the option is now exhausted —
   in which case an appeal must acknowledge a prior review rather than ask for a first one.
2. The ban was applied on 8 Aug and he only discovered it on 15 Aug.
3. The date refers to something else entirely in WhatsApp's flow.

**Ask Gaurang directly: did he already request a review, and if so when and what came back?**
Writing to Meta describing this as a first appeal, when a review was already declined, would
undermine the letter immediately.

## Likely cause

Gaurang believes it was caused by sending messages containing donation links and QR codes.
That is close, but the mechanism is worth being precise about, because it shapes both the
appeal and how the campaign operates from here.

WhatsApp's automated systems act on **behavioural signals**, not on content. The pattern that
triggers a ban is:

- the same or near-identical message sent to **many recipients in a short window**
- a high proportion of those recipients **not being in the sender's address book**
- messages containing **links**, forwarded repeatedly
- and decisively, recipients pressing **Block** or **Report**

A grieving father forwarding an appeal to hundreds of people produces exactly this
signature. The system cannot distinguish it from spam, because at the level it observes,
it is indistinguishable.
