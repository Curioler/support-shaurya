## Disclaimer (verbatim, page 3)

> - The MLPA test will not detect the point mutations in the *DMD* gene, which are the
>   second most common cause of genetic defects in the *DMD* gene. It is therefore
>   recommended to use MLPA in combination with sequence analysis.
> - A point mutation or polymorphism in the sequence detected by a probe, which results in
>   reduced probe binding efficiency, can also cause a reduction in relative peak area.
>   **Therefore, single exon deletions detected by MLPA should always be confirmed by other
>   methods like multiplex PCR or sequencing.**
> - MLPA cannot detect any changes that lie outside the target sequence of the probes and
>   will not detect most inversions or translocations.
