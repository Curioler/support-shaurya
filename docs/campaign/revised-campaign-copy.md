## ImpactGuru — replacement story *(Gaurang's voice)*

```
My name is Gaurang K. Nayak, and I am writing this with a father's heart, filled with
fear and hope at the same time.

My son Shaurya is four years old. At an age when children should be running, laughing
and starting school, my little boy is fighting Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy - a rare
genetic disease that slowly takes away the strength of every muscle in the body,
including the heart and the muscles used to breathe.

On 1st May 2026, our world changed. A blood test showed his CPK level was 22,051,
against a normal range of 75 to 230 for his age. Genetic testing at MedGenome Labs,

Today Shaurya still walks. He still climbs stairs, though it is becoming harder, and he

disease takes a little more.

Shaurya is under the care of Dr Ann Agnes Mathew, Consultant Paediatric Neurologist at
SPARSH Hospital, Bengaluru, alongside doctors in Vadodara and Pune. On 9th July 2026,

In her written certificate, she says something that will not leave me:

    "I recommend that the above-mentioned medicine be imported for saving the life of
    this patient as soon as possible, as the medicine works in a time limited fashion

That is why we cannot wait. This treatment works best while a child can still walk. For
Shaurya, that window is open now.

ELEVIDYS is not manufactured in India and must be imported. The cost is approximately
US$3.2 million - around Rs. 26 crore, excluding taxes and import duties.

I work in fire and safety, and I am the only earning member of our family. We have
already spent everything we had on diagnostic tests and on the physiotherapy Shaurya
needs every week. Rs. 26 crore is a number I cannot even picture. But next to my son's
life, it is only a number.

So today I am setting aside my pride and asking for your help.

Every contribution matters, whether it is Rs. 500 or Rs. 5,000. And if you cannot give,
please share our fundraiser with your family, friends and colleagues. One share can
reach the one person who is able to help.

All our medical documents - the genetic report, the doctors' prescriptions and the
treating physician's certificate - have been submitted and verified on this platform.
We have nothing to hide. We only have a son we are trying to save.

Thank you for reading this far, and for standing with us.

With gratitude,
Gaurang K. Nayak
Father of Shaurya
```

# PART 3 — What changed, and why

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| **"My name is Jolly Gaurang Nayak"** on Ketto | The live page says *"My name is Shaurya Nayak, and I am fighting to save my son's life"* — that is the four-year-old patient's name, in his mother's appeal |
| **Dr Mathew's quote added to both** | The strongest sentence the campaign has, and it was going unused. A named consultant attesting urgency in writing beats any amount of adjectives |
| **CPK 22,051 and the MedGenome confirmation added** | Specific, checkable facts. They turn "please believe us" into "here is the evidence", which is what moves larger donors and journalists |
| **"Excluding taxes and import duties" on both** | Already on Ketto, missing from ImpactGuru. Honest that the target is a floor |
| **"Slow the disease", never "cure"** | ELEVIDYS is not curative. Donors and journalists check high-profile therapies, and a correction on this point would cast doubt on everything else |
| **The "₹1 lakh" figure removed** | It read oddly against "exhausted our savings" and invited doubt. Replaced with what the money actually went on — diagnostics and weekly physiotherapy |
| **"He still walks" made central** | True, urgent, and what makes the deadline real rather than rhetorical |
| **Verification mentioned explicitly on ImpactGuru** | The platform's verified badges are an asset the copy never used |
| **"No cure" line dropped from Ketto** | The original said "There is no cure" two paragraphs before describing a treatment. Accurate but self-defeating — the point is that something *can* be done, and quickly |
